SECTION 3 — Unauthorized access to information system or data
Whoever gains unauthorized access to any information system or data is punished with
imprisonment which may extend to three months, or with fine which may extend to fifty
thousand rupees, or with both.

SECTION 4 — Unauthorized copying or transmission of data
Whoever, with dishonest intention, copies or otherwise transmits data without authorization
is punished with imprisonment which may extend to six months, or with fine which may extend
to one hundred thousand rupees, or with both.

SECTION 10-A — Cyber terrorism
An act committed with the intent to glorify an offence relating to terrorism, or any person
convicted of such an offence, through an information system, is treated as cyber terrorism
and is punishable under the Act with the most severe penalties it provides, including
imprisonment for life in aggravated circumstances.

SECTION 11 — Hate speech
A person who prepares or disseminates information, through any information system, that
advances or is likely to advance interfaith, sectarian, or racial hatred is punished with
imprisonment which may extend to seven years, or with fine, or with both.

SECTION 20 — Offences against dignity of a natural person
Whoever intentionally and publicly exhibits or displays or transmits information through
an information system that harms the reputation or dignity of a natural person, or amounts
to violation of privacy, is punished with imprisonment which may extend to three years, or
with fine which may extend to one million rupees, or with both.

SECTION 21 — Offences against modesty of a natural person and minor
Whoever intentionally and publicly displays, distributes, or transmits information that
superimposes a photograph or video of a person's face onto sexually explicit content, or
otherwise intimidates or harasses a person through an information system with sexual
content, is punished with imprisonment which may extend to five years, or with fine which
may extend to five million rupees, or with both; enhanced penalties apply where the victim
is a minor.

SECTION 24 — Cyberstalking
A person commits the offence of cyberstalking who, with the intent to coerce, intimidate,
or harass, follows another person, contacts them, or watches or spies on them through an
information system in a manner that causes fear or distress. Punishment may extend to
imprisonment of three years, or fine, or both, with enhanced penalties where the victim is
a minor.

SECTION 37 — Unlawful online content
The Pakistan Telecommunication Authority is empowered to remove or block access to any
online content it considers to be unlawful, including content that is against the glory
of Islam, the security of Pakistan, public order, decency or morality, or contempt of court.
