SECTION 299 — Definitions
Defines key terms used throughout the Code, including "culpable homicide," "death,"
"hurt," and "wrongful act," which form the basis for later offence definitions.

SECTION 302 — Punishment of Qatl-i-Amd (intentional murder)
Whoever commits murder is punished, subject to the provisions of the Code, with death as
qisas, or with death or imprisonment for life as ta'zir, having regard to the facts and
circumstances of the case, and may also be liable to a fine.

SECTION 320 — Qatl-i-khata (causing death by mistake)
Where death is caused by a mistake of act or of intention, or both, the offence is treated
as qatl-i-khata, punishable in accordance with the provisions relating to diyat (compensation)
under the Code.

SECTION 375 — Rape
Defines the offence of rape, including the circumstances under which sexual intercourse by
a man with a woman is considered to be against her will, without her consent, or otherwise
unlawful under the conditions set out in the section.

SECTION 379 — Punishment for theft
Whoever commits theft is punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which
may extend to three years, or with fine, or with both.

SECTION 392 — Punishment for robbery
Whoever commits robbery is punished with rigorous imprisonment for a term which may extend
to ten years, and is also liable to fine; if the robbery is committed on the highway between
sunset and sunrise, the imprisonment may extend to fourteen years.

SECTION 406 — Punishment for criminal breach of trust
Whoever commits criminal breach of trust is punished with imprisonment of either description
for a term which may extend to three years, or with fine, or with both.

SECTION 420 — Cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property
Whoever cheats and thereby dishonestly induces the person deceived to deliver any property,
or to make, alter, or destroy any valuable security, is punished with imprisonment of either
description for a term which may extend to seven years, and is also liable to fine.

SECTION 495 — Marrying again during the lifetime of a husband or wife
Whoever, having a spouse living, marries again without lawful authority for doing so, and
conceals from the person with whom the subsequent marriage is contracted the fact of the
former marriage, is punished with imprisonment which may extend to ten years, and is also
liable to fine.

SECTION 506 — Punishment for criminal intimidation
Whoever commits criminal intimidation is punished with imprisonment of either description
for a term which may extend to two years, or with fine, or with both; if the threat is to
cause death or grievous hurt, the punishment may extend to seven years.
